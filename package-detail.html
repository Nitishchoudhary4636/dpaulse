Overview

Paris & Zurich have always been at the top of a world traveller’s bucket list, especially for couples and families. Begin your vacation in glamorous Paris, exploring the romantic River Seine and taking pictures before the iconic Eiffel Tower. Then journey to Switzerland, spending four unforgettable nights by the Alpine forests and lakes of Zurich, ascending Mt. Titlis, and experiencing Rhine Falls & Black Forest in Germany.

Day-by-Day Detailed Itinerary
Day 1: Welcome to Paris & Hotel Check-in
Welcome to the fashion and romance capital of the world—Paris! Upon landing at Charles de Gaulle Airport, meet our DPauls representative and transfer to your 4-star hotel. In the evening, enjoy a warm gourmet Indian dinner. Overnight stay in Paris.
Meals Included: Dinner
Day 2: Eiffel Tower 2nd Level, Guided City Tour & Seine Cruise
After breakfast, ascend to the 2nd level of the Eiffel Tower for spectacular 360-degree panoramic views of Paris. Continue on a guided city tour seeing Arc de Triomphe, Champs Élysées, Louvre Pyramid, and Notre-Dame. Enjoy a romantic 1-hour cruise along River Seine.
Meals Included: Breakfast + Dinner
Day 3: Scenic Drive from Paris to Zurich, Switzerland
Check out and board our luxury coach as we drive past the picturesque French countryside and Swiss Alpine borders into Zurich. Arrive in Zurich, check into your 4-star hotel and enjoy an Indian dinner.
Meals Included: Breakfast + Dinner
Day 4: Majestic Rhine Falls & Black Forest Excursion
Visit Rhine Falls in Schaffhausen, Europe's largest waterfall, experiencing the roar of water. Cross into Germany's Black Forest to visit Drubba in the Titisee region to witness traditional Cuckoo clock craftsmanship.
Meals Included: Breakfast + Dinner
Day 5: Mt. Titlis Cable Car, Ice Flyer & Lucerne Orientation
Ascend Mt. Titlis in the world's first revolving cable car 'Titlis Rotair' to 10,000 feet. Walk on the Titlis Cliff Bridge and ride the Ice Flyer chairlift over glaciers. In the afternoon, tour historic Lucerne, visiting the Lion Monument and Chapel Bridge.
Meals Included: Breakfast + Dinner
Day 6: Scenic Interlaken & Grindelwald Alpine Village
Spend the day exploring Grindelwald village nestled under the North Face of the Eiger and Interlaken nestled between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz. Shop for authentic Swiss chocolates and watches before an Indian dinner.
Meals Included: Breakfast + Dinner
Day 7: Zurich Airport Departure • Fly Back Home
Enjoy breakfast, check out from the hotel, and take your private transfer to Zurich Airport for your non-stop return flight to India with unforgettable memories!
Meals Included: Breakfast
